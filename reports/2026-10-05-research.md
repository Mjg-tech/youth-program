# Research report: 5 October 2026 (first cycle)

First cycle of the Youth Program database. 60 records: 41 found in this cycle (internships and youth
programmes for people with little or no experience, Kinshasa first) and 19 youth-oriented records
reused from the sister project `global-opportunities`, where they had been read on the organisers'
own pages (batch files in `research/2026-10-05/`). One programme (KOICA Youth Leaders, not open to
the DRC) went straight to the archive.

## Verification summary

Most records found in this cycle are **NEEDS VERIFICATION**: they come from job boards and
aggregators, and several official sites blocked the reader (afdb.org and fundsforNGOs returned HTTP
403, youth.cd shows an expired certificate, app.unv.org stopped loading after one read). The reused
records keep their original verification labels.

- **World Vision administration internship:** no date; requires a Bac+5; may be closed.
